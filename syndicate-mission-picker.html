Mission Reader: see the best mission right in the game

Mission Reader is a bookmark. Click it while you play and a small panel shows which of the 3 missions gives you the most for your energy. You set it up once, which takes about a minute. It works on a computer in Chrome, Edge, Brave or Opera.

Set it up (once)

  1. Make your bookmarks bar visible: press Ctrl+Shift+B. A bar appears under the address bar. On a Mac, press Cmd+Shift+B.
  2. Hold down the mouse on this red button and drag it up onto that bar. Let go there. Mission Reader
  3. Done. You now have a Mission Reader button in your bookmarks bar.
Dragging doesn't work? Do this instead
  1. Click Copy code below.
  2. Right-click an empty spot on your bookmarks bar and choose Add page (in some browsers it's called Add bookmark).
  3. For the name, type Mission Reader. Click in the URL box, delete what's there and press Ctrl+V to paste. Click Save.

Use it

  1. Open the game and go to the Godfather bar.
  2. Click Mission Reader in your bookmarks bar. The panel appears in the top-right corner.
  3. The big red name at the top is the mission to pick. Use the buttons to choose what you care about: XP for leveling up, Cash for money, Balanced for both.

Good to know

  • You only click the bookmark once per visit. The panel hides by itself when you leave the Godfather bar and comes back when you return.
  • If you reload the game page (F5) or open it again later, click the bookmark again.
  • A tag like Gold or Legendary next to a name means that mission also gives an item. The higher the tier, the better the item: Item, Gold, Epic, Legendary, Unique. Epic and better ones glow.
  • Drag the panel by its top edge to move it. – makes it small, ◐ switches between dark and light, × closes it.
